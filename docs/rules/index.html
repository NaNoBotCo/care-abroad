LawRules

Some people travel because treatment is cheaper elsewhere. Others travel because it is lawful elsewhere. This grid is the second kind: one question down the side, one jurisdiction across the top, and every cell a reading of one named instrument on one date. Hover a cell for the instrument and the date it was read. Nothing here is legal advice.
