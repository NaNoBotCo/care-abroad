Two reasons people fly

One is money: the operation costs less somewhere else, or the queue at home is fourteen months long. The other is law: the treatment is prohibited at home and lawful across a border. The prices page is the first. The rules grid is the second.

The number under the number

A joint package priced at 356,500 baht excludes the implant, and the implant is often the largest line on the bill. Every price here carries the provider's own exclusion list, because that is where the difference between two quotes usually lives.
