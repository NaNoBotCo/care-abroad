What is missing, said plainly

  • field observations — no record carries the field tier
  • most of the world's hospitals; the written-up ones are a sample, not a census
  • outcome data per unit outside the few registries that publish it
  • complication and revision rates for work done abroad, which almost nobody counts
  • prices in most countries, because most hospitals publish none
  • photographs for most records

A procedure with no price here is one nobody has published a figure for where this project could read it. It is not a free procedure and not a rare one.

A blank cell is 'nobody here has read that law', which is a fact about this project. Statutes change between a reading and a reader.

A hospital missing here is missing from OpenStreetMap on the fetch date, outside the radius queried, or not yet written up.
