TÜRKİYE — WHAT WAS READ OFF THE PRIMARY SOURCES, 17 SEPTEMBER 2026
=================================================================
Filed here because two of these correct things most secondary writing on the subject
still gets wrong, and because the rule record turkiye-health-tourism-authorisation was
being drafted from the older instrument.

1. THE 2017 REGULATION IS REPEALED
   In force: Uluslararası Sağlık Turizmi ve Turistin Sağlığı Hakkında Yönetmelik,
   Resmî Gazete 26 April 2025, Sayı 32882. MADDE 17 repeals the 13/7/2017 regulation
   (RG 30123). Anything citing 30123 as current is out of date.

   What it requires:
     MADDE 5(1)  the authorisation certificate is compulsory for the facility AND the
                 intermediary — Ministry licenses the facility, USHAŞ the intermediary
     MADDE 5(5)  non-transferable; 5(4) a separate certificate per branch
     MADDE 6(1)(b) TÜSKA accreditation for hospitals, medical centres, medical
                 laboratories and dialysis centres; deadline 31/12/2026
     MADDE 6(1)(c) complication insurance compulsory for theatre procedures;
                 deadline 31/12/2025
     MADDE 6(1)(e) a facility may not buy intermediary services from an unlicensed party
     MADDE 6(3)  complications and medical malpractice expressly inside the holder's
                 responsibility
     MADDE 7     an international health tourism unit with a documented foreign-language
                 speaker; agencies need 24/7 capacity in at least two languages
     MADDE 8(4)  registration on the HealthTürkiye portal is compulsory
     MADDE 11(1) pricing is set by the Ministry
     MADDE 9     annual performance evaluation by USHAŞ; 3-month then 6-month suspension,
                 cancellation after a failed second control evaluation
     MADDE 16    cancellation for a year of inactivity; no new certificate for a year

2. HAIR TRANSPLANTS HAVE THEIR OWN RULE
   Saç Ekimi Birimleri Hakkında Yönetmelik, RG 6 May 2023, Sayı 32182 (amended 3 Nov 2023
   and Sept 2025).
     MADDE 9(3)   only physicians holding the applicator certificate may perform the
                  channel-opening stage
     MADDE 9(4)   assistants work only under a certified physician
     MADDE 9(6)   one certified physician covers at most five rooms
     MADDE 10(3)  every case logged with technique, graft count, region, before and
                  72-hour photographs, and the names of the team — with one carve-out:
                  the six-month photograph is not required for inbound health-tourism
                  patients
     MADDE 10(4)  one patient per room at a time
     MADDE 11(5)  fine of 1–3% of the previous month's gross service revenue; on
                  persistence the activity certificate is cancelled with no re-issue
     MADDE 11(1)  inspection at least once a year, plus unscheduled on complaint
   Public register: sacekimmerkezi.saglik.gov.tr (MADDE 12(2)).

   Read for the record: the 2023 prohibitions map one-for-one onto the practices ISHRS
   had been alleging since 2016. That is regulatory acknowledgement, and it is better
   evidence than any trade-press assertion.

3. THE OFFICIAL SERIES, AND WHAT IT COUNTS
   TÜİK, Departing Visitors Survey, table "Geliş Nedenine Göre Çıkış Yapan Ziyaretçiler",
   column "Sağlık ve tıbbi nedenler (1 yıldan az)". A border exit survey of STATED
   PURPOSE. Not a count of patients treated, not a count of people.

     2019   756,926        2023  1,538,643
     2020   435,691 *      2024  1,506,442
     2021   729,592        2025  1,398,580  (of which 250,401 Turkish citizens resident abroad)
     2022 1,381,807        2026 H1 563,327
     * Q2 2020 not published: no border surveys during the pandemic.

   USHAŞ republishes those counts and adds an income series, attributing both to TÜİK.
   Income: 2019 USD 1.459bn · 2023 USD 3.006bn · 2024 USD 3.023bn · 2025 USD 3.022bn ·
   2026 H1 USD 1.580bn. The person counts match TÜİK exactly, so USHAŞ and TÜİK are one
   source, not two.

   Per-visitor spend: NOT PUBLISHED by anyone. Dividing the two gives about USD 2,160,
   which is arithmetic and is labelled as such wherever it appears here.

4. HOW MANY FACILITIES HOLD THE CERTIFICATE
   Counted from the Ministry's four published lists, all stamped 20 August 2026:
     hospitals 542 · medical centres 135 · physician offices 2,468 · other 1,581.
   Total 4,726, which is a sum made here; the Ministry prints no total.
   The count of authorised intermediaries is not published anywhere that could be read.

5. TWO CIRCULATING FIGURES THIS CONTRADICTS
   "Türkiye's health tourism income topped $4.5 billion in 2015" (TÜRSAB, via ISHRS 2016)
   cannot be reconciled with the official series, which does not reach USD 3bn until 2023.
   "About US$1 billion a year" for hair transplants (Haider et al. 2025, Aesthetic Plastic
   Surgery) appears in a peer-reviewed journal but traces to a 2015 magazine article. The
   same review calls the sector a data black hole, which is the part worth carrying.

6. NOT SOURCED — leave empty rather than fill
   TÜİK-published health income or per-visitor spend as a TÜİK table · a published count
   of authorised intermediaries · a peer-reviewed count of hair-restoration travel to
   Türkiye · a Türk Tabipleri Birliği statement on unlicensed technicians · a published
   count of clinics closed by inspection · any split of the 1.4m figure by procedure.
