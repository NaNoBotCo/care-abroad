CARE ABROAD — HOW TO WRITE A RECORD, AND THE CAST LIST
======================================================
One JSON file per node, at data/nodes/<type>/<id>.json. The id is the filename. The
schema is schema/node.schema.json and validate.py enforces it.

Read the three hand-written exemplars before writing anything:
  data/nodes/procedure/knee-replacement.json   depth, price block, inline source marks
  data/nodes/facility/bumrungrad.json          metrics, tags, recognitions, geo
  data/nodes/dataset/world-bank-health-spending.json   what a series counts, and does not


THE FIELDS
----------
schema        always 1
id            kebab-case, matches the filename
type          matches the folder
names         name · aliases[] · said (the local name, or how the trade says it)
etymology     root · first_attested · note · tier · source. Words and procedures earn one;
              a hospital does not.
region        one or more keys from data/vocab/regions.json
facets        per type, keys from data/vocab/facets.json. facets.iso2 is ISO 3166-1
              alpha-2 and is the join key for every map, chart and table on the site.
text.what     one paragraph. What it is. No preamble.
text.story    the narrative, told as narrative. Mark each stretch inline:
              plain prose = cited · *Trade practice —* · *Tradition holds —* · *Inference —*
text.how      how it is done, priced, regulated or travelled
text.today    present tense, dated
kin           what other nodes ARE to this one, said from here. See KIN below.
geo           lat/lon + precision (exact, street, city, region, country) + source
address       street · city · area · country (ISO-2) · postcode
metrics       numbers the record states about itself, each with as_of and source. A number
              nobody published stays null and prints as a gap.
prices        see MONEY below
legality      see LAW below
tags          keys from data/vocab/tags.json; each names its source. Tier cited,
              harvested or field — the validator refuses tradition and inference here.
recognitions  accreditations and listings; `by` from data/vocab/recognizers.json
dataset       for type dataset only: publisher, unit, counts, does_not_count
sources       ids from data/sources/sources.json
provenance    default tier + per-field overrides
confidence    high / medium / low  ·  needs_verification: true where you are unsure
updated       the date you wrote it


THE THIRTEEN TYPES
------------------
destination  a country as a place people are treated in, or leave. direction: inbound,
             outbound or both. A country people mostly LEAVE is as much a record as one
             they arrive in, and the outflow is half the subject.
hub          a city or a district — a street of dental surgeries, a hospital quarter, a
             corridor of hair clinics. facets.radius_km sets how far tools/harvest_osm.py
             queries around its point.
procedure    an operation or a course of treatment. Carries prices[].
facility     a named hospital or clinic. Carries metrics, tags, recognitions, prices[].
pathway      one step of the journey, from deciding to complaining. facets.stage orders them.
risk         what goes wrong, who measures it, who pays.
rule         a visa class, a licensing regime, an entitlement, a prohibition, a
             reimbursement route. Carries legality[] where it maps across countries.
org          an accreditor, a ministry, a trade body, a registry, a society.
person       a surgeon, a founder, a regulator, a researcher, a patient who wrote it down.
event        a conference, a ruling, an outbreak, a year the field turned.
term         a word, with its root and the fight over it where there is one.
dataset      a series this site draws a chart from. The does_not_count sentence is the point.
story        a longer piece.


KIN IS THE SPINE
----------------
Each kin entry says what the other node IS to this one, in one sentence a reader standing
on this page would want. Not "see also". The other record answers in its own words, so
write yours from here. Link only to ids on the cast list. 5–12 kin on a rich record, 3–6
on a small one. Reach across batches freely — another agent is writing that record now.


MONEY
-----
A price row is a number somebody PUBLISHED, with its currency, its date, and what it
covers. Not a quote you were given, not an average, not a range with no source.

  kind    package      a fixed bundle off a provider's own page
          list         a single-item list price
          tariff       a state or insurer schedule
          reimbursement what a payer refunds
          survey       a published survey or study figure
          estimate     a published estimate, method named in the note
          quoted-range what a provider advertises as "from X to Y"
  amount / amount_high  the range, low first
  currency ISO 4217. Leave usd alone — build.py computes it from one dated rate table.
  as_of    YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY. A price without a date fails validation.
  includes / excludes   the provider's own words. The exclusion list is usually where
          the story is: a joint package that excludes the implant is quoting half a bill.

Read the price off the page yourself and register that page as a source. Where the page
is rendered by script and WebFetch returns a shell, use the browser tools.


LAW
---
A legality row is a reading of ONE named instrument on ONE date.
  status  lawful · lawful-with-conditions · residents-only · unlawful · unregulated · no-data
  instrument  the act, article, directive or ministerial regulation, named
  as_of   the date of the reading. Law moves; an undated row cannot be read.
Put legality[] on the rule record for a cross-border question (surrogacy, donor anonymity,
assisted dying, transplant), not on every procedure. Nothing on this site is legal advice
and no record says what a reader should do.


VOICE
-----
BANNED, hard error (validate.py).            [stylecheck: allow-start]
  load-bearing · honest · authentic · inauthentic · purist · tourist · the real thing
  world-class · state-of-the-art · cutting-edge · top-rated · award-winning · risk-free
  pain-free · hassle-free · life-changing · miracle · breakthrough treatment
  guaranteed results · save up to · savings of up to · up to N% cheaper
  we recommend · you should choose/book/go/travel/have · your best option
  don't worry · rest assured · peace of mind   [stylecheck: allow-end]
A quotation in quotation marks is exempt, so quote and attribute instead of asserting.

The sixth word on that list is the field's own name for itself. So this site writes
medical travel and cross-border care, and the two-word phrase built on that sixth word
appears in quotation marks with a speaker, or on the word's own page, where the fight
over it is the subject.

This subject runs on argument — cheaper against riskier, queue against fee, the state that
will not pay against the state that will not allow. REPORT every argument. JOIN none.
A back-street hair clinic gets described the same way a university hospital does.

Write short. Lead with a verb. Cut "the" and "what" wherever the sentence survives it.
Fewer adjectives, more verbs. Define every non-obvious word in a clause at first use —
DRG, graft, cycle, tariff, ASA grade, day case. No sentence that announces the next
sentence. No closing summary. Every price and every number carries its date.

This is a directory, not a clinic. It describes; it does not advise. No second person
telling a reader what to do about their own body.


PICTURES
--------
images[] stays empty. Put candidate Wikimedia Commons file titles in
x_commons_files: ["File:...jpg"] and a later pass fetches them with their licences.
The visuals on this site are drawn from the data, not photographed.


FINISH BY
---------
  BUILD_DRAFT=1 python3 tools/validate.py
Fix every ERROR naming one of YOUR records. Kin-target warnings for records other agents
are still writing are expected.
